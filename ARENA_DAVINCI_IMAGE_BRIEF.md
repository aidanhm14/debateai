# Arena image brief for DaVinci

## Objective

Create documentary debate photography that makes Debatable feel inhabited by normal people. The images should look observed, not art-directed. They are supporting evidence that real conversations happen here, not hero art.

Do not create studio headshots or polished profile portraits. Do not make people look like generated brand ambassadors.

## Visual rules

- Photograph a moment, not a pose.
- Favor real debate environments: classrooms, bedrooms, libraries, community halls, laptop webcams, folding tables, notes, timers, and water bottles.
- Keep normal imperfections: uneven practical lighting, slight webcam noise, fabric wrinkles, flyaway hair, imperfect posture, and lived-in rooms.
- Use ordinary smart-casual clothing. Avoid coordinated wardrobes, luxury styling, and startup-ad smiles.
- Faces should be expressive because someone is listening, thinking, disagreeing, or speaking. Nobody looks directly into a studio camera unless the image is a webcam frame.
- Include a range of ages, skin tones, body types, and personal styles without arranging people into an obviously engineered diversity lineup.
- Use shallow depth of field only when a real camera would produce it. Webcam images should have normal deep focus.
- Leave UI-safe negative space only where requested.
- No text, logos, watermarks, flags, fake school crests, microphones on a stage, neon lighting, holograms, sci-fi overlays, or glowing AI motifs.

## Anti-AI texture

Reject any result with:

- waxy or poreless skin;
- identical smiles or symmetrical poses;
- perfect teeth and catchlights on every subject;
- overly clean rooms;
- cinematic orange-and-teal grading;
- floating objects, malformed hands, repeated people, or unreadable papers;
- a stock-photo meeting-table composition;
- a person centered against a plain studio background;
- fake interface elements baked into the image.

## Prompt 1: Arena broadcast room

> Documentary photograph of a university debate practice in a normal classroom. One student is standing near a simple lectern delivering a speech while two teams sit at separate tables, listening and taking notes. Laptops, legal pads, pens, water bottles, backpacks, and a visible wall clock make the room feel used. Fluorescent ceiling lights mixed with window light, realistic skin texture, ordinary smart-casual clothes, slightly imperfect framing as if captured by a student in the back row. The speaker is animated but not posing. Wide 16:9 composition with enough breathing room for a small interface overlay. Natural color, restrained contrast, subtle camera noise. No text, no logos, no stage lighting, no audience applause, no cinematic grading, no watermark.

## Prompt 2: Remote speaker frames

Generate these as separate images, not a contact sheet.

> A candid laptop-webcam frame of a young adult participating in a serious online debate from an ordinary bedroom or study space. The person is mid-argument, focused on the screen, wearing everyday clothes and basic earbuds or headphones. Slightly uneven lamp and monitor light, normal webcam sharpness, mild digital noise, realistic skin and hair, a few ordinary objects in the background. The composition should look like a real video-call capture, not a portrait session. Landscape 4:3. No text, no logos, no beauty retouching, no perfect background, no exaggerated expression, no watermark.

Create six distinct frames with different rooms, lighting conditions, ages, appearances, and speaking or listening moments. Do not reuse a face or room.

## Prompt 3: Debate TV venue

> Observational photograph from the side aisle of a crowded public debate in a traditional hall. The audience is seated closely and watching a speaker who is visible but not theatrically spotlighted. People lean forward, whisper, take notes, and react naturally. Mixed practical room lighting, deep reds and warm wood, modest camera grain, slight motion blur on one gesturing hand. The framing should feel like a journalist arrived during the event, not like an advertisement. Wide 16:9 crop. No text, no logos, no perfect symmetry, no dramatic movie lighting, no watermark.

## Prompt 4: Small community round

> Candid photo of four people debating around two pushed-together classroom tables in a library meeting room. One person is speaking with a hand gesture, two are listening and flowing on paper, and one is preparing a response on a laptop. The room is practical and slightly cluttered. Daylight, overhead lights, ordinary clothes, natural expressions, realistic skin texture, documentary composition. Wide 3:2 crop. No text, no logos, no staged smiles, no corporate-meeting look, no watermark.

## Delivery

- Export each image separately as a high-quality JPEG.
- Hero and venue: 2048 by 1152 or larger, 16:9.
- Remote speaker frames: 1600 by 1200 or larger, 4:3.
- Community round: 1800 by 1200 or larger, 3:2.
- Keep the original uncropped output alongside the web crop.
- Do not add names, ratings, debate sides, or UI labels to the image. The product renders those separately.

## Selection test

Show the image without the Debatable interface and ask:

1. Could this plausibly be a frame from a real debate?
2. Does anyone look selected for an advertisement?
3. Are there enough ordinary details to make the room believable?
4. Would a normal person see peers rather than an AI aesthetic?

If the second answer is yes or the fourth answer is no, reject the image.
