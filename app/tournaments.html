Straight answers

The questions people actually ask.

Anything not covered here lives in the official rules. Still stuck? Email aidandavidhollinger@gmail.com and you get an answer from the person running it.

I have never debated. Am I going to embarrass myself?

Most people who enter have never competed. The format is written for them: no jargon, no prepared case, no citations to memorise. You make a point, explain why it holds, and answer what the other person said. You will lose some rounds, everyone does, and the judge writes its reasoning in plain English, so a round you lose still tells you exactly what to fix.

There is prize money. Where does it come from?

We fund them, so the ladder is fixed whether eight people enter or eight hundred. Entry is free and there is no fee to waive. The one condition on the cash is age: you confirm you are 18 or over when you enter, and if you are under 18 you play the same field for the placement and the ranking while that cash moves to the next entrant who is. Tips of $5 are accepted if you want to chip in, and a tip changes nothing about your pairings, your ballots or your standing.

Who judges, and is that actually fair?

Rounds are judged by an AI judge working from a rubric that is published before the round, not written after it. It gives a written reason for every decision, and either debater can appeal, at which point a human reviews it. The criteria, the reliability figures, and the appeal process are all public at the judge integrity page. Nobody has to take our word for how a round was decided.

The final is the one exception. It is decided by the people watching: voting opens when the last speech ends and anyone signed in gets one vote, with the AI ballot published beside it. The official rules carry the full version.

What if I enter and then cannot make it?

Nothing happens. There is no set start you can miss, so an entry you never use costs you nothing and costs nobody else a round. If you paid in and the day falls through entirely, ask before entries close and you get the fee back.

Will I be on a stream?

Only if you make it deep into the bracket. Preliminary rounds are not broadcast. Elimination rounds, including the final, may be streamed and recorded, and entering counts as agreeing to that. If being on camera is a problem, say so before the event and it can be worked around.

Can I just watch?

Yes, and it costs nothing. Live rounds and past ones are on the watch page, no account required. Watching a couple of rounds is the cheapest way to decide whether you want to enter the next one.

Question, team, or your own tournament?

One inbox, answered by the person running it. Bring a club and we enter you together and keep you off each other early where we can.
