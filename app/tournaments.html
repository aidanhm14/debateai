    Straight answers

    The questions people actually ask.

    Anything not covered here lives in the official rules. Still stuck? Email hello@itsdebatable.com and you get an answer from the person running it.

    I have never debated. Am I going to embarrass myself?

    Most people who enter have never done this before. New Debatable rounds use a casual 1v1 with no format jargon, prepared case, or tournament rulebook. You make a point, explain why it holds, and answer what the other person said. The judge writes its reasoning in plain English and scores both people out of 100.

    There is prize money. Where does it come from?

    We fund them, so the ladder is fixed whether eight people enter or eight hundred. Entry is free and there is no fee to waive. Everyone competes in one field. We encourage entrants to be 18+. Cash prizes can only be paid to eligible winners aged 18 or over, verified before payout.

    Who judges, and is that actually fair?

    Rounds are judged by an AI judge working from a rubric that is published before the round, not written after it. It gives a written reason for every decision, and either debater can appeal, at which point a human reviews it. The criteria, the reliability figures, and the appeal process are all public at the judge integrity page. Nobody has to take our word for how a round was decided.

    The final is the one exception. It is decided by the people watching: voting opens when the last speech ends and anyone signed in gets one vote, with the AI ballot published beside it. The official rules carry the full version.

    What if I enter and then cannot make it?

    Nothing is charged. If you do not appear, you are not paired. The roster locks when the tournament starts, but a person already registered may join the pairing pool later while preliminary rounds are still running.

    Will I be on a stream?

    Every tournament round is recorded, including questions and interruptions. Preliminary footage and generated clips are private by default; elimination rounds, including the final, may be streamed under the official rules. A concise, relevant question can help your score, while heckling can hurt it. The AI receives calculated pace and flags spreading above 250 WPM. Camera adds 2 standings points, Avatar subtracts 1, and Off or dark subtracts 3. Camera mode never changes the round winner. Registering, checking in, and entering the room each make these rules clear before you play. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must approve. If you cannot be recorded at all, do not enter.

    Can I just watch?

    Yes, and it costs nothing. Live rounds and past ones are on the watch page, no account required. Watching a couple of rounds is the cheapest way to decide whether you want to enter the next one.
