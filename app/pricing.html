Pricing FAQ

What does it cost right now?

Free gives you one voice round and a handful of typed ones. Individual is $10 for the year. Voice is $12 a month and lifts the voice cap. BYOK is $1 a month if you bring your own Claude key. Team is $50 a year. Tokens is $4.99 a month if you only want more voice rounds. School programs start with a conversation.

Why is it so cheap?

The first paid buyers are students, coaches, and clubs. Typed practice is annual on purpose, so it feels closer to a tournament fee than a monthly software bill. Voice is the exception. It is monthly because a live voice round costs us real money to run, and pricing it like the typed tier would mean losing money on everyone who actually used it.

I coach a team. What should I budget?

Budget $550 for the season license. It covers the whole roster, unlimited judged rounds, coach administration, invoicing, and privacy paperwork. Team is a study-group plan, not the school offer.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep full access until the end of your billing period, no mid-tournament lockout. After that you drop to the free plan. One click from the app's Team tab.

Does it actually make me better?

You give timed speeches, hear real pushback, and leave with a judge ballot that names what decided the round. Each supported format follows its own structure and evidence rules.

Is my data used to train models?

Your cases are never sent to GPT, Claude, or any third-party model for training. Signed-in, rated work can feed Debatable's own learning loop. Delete your data anytime from the Team tab.
