# AI button invitations

Recorded on 2026-09-10 with OpenAI `gpt-live-1`, voice `marin`, through
the primary Live WebSocket API. These are fixed promotional clips, not
live sessions with visitors. No microphone is requested for playback.

- `hover.mp3`: “Hey, wanna debate me?”
- `alone.mp3`: “Wanna debate me? Press Debate the AI, and get a ranking.”

The returned transcripts were checked against both exact lines before
encoding to MP3. Playback follows the site's sound mute setting and browser
autoplay policy. The automatic invitation is once per tab session, only
after successful activity reads show at most one online visitor, no other
waiting person, no watchable round, and no site stream. Missing, failed,
or stale activity reads do not qualify. AI ranks are the solo judged-score
board; this invitation does not change ranking rules.
