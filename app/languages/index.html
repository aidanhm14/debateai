Questions

Multilingual debate FAQ

Which languages can I use for debate practice?

The full prep interface and AI output support English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Simplified Chinese, and Korean. Voice rounds also handle additional spoken languages, including Arabic and Bengali.

Will the AI judge respond in the same language?

Yes. Your selected language applies to generated cases, rebuttals, feedback, section headings, and the written judge ballot. Proper names can remain in their original form.

Can two people debate in different languages?

Yes, in live human rounds that use translated captions. Each person speaks normally, and the other side can read a translated caption in a supported language.

Does debate terminology get translated?

The system keeps debate terms intact when a circuit normally uses the borrowed term, such as motion, POI, rebuttal, or speaker points. The rest of the explanation follows the language you selected.
