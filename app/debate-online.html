What you can debate online here

Debatable supports 15 formats across school, college, casual, and professional argument. Each mode has its own speech order, timing, citation rules, and judging criteria.

  • Asian Parliamentary · 3-on-3, POIs, and reply speeches
  • British Parliamentary (BP) · 4 teams, opening and closing extensions
  • APDA · US college parli, impromptu, no fake citations
  • Worlds (WUDC) · full four-team Worlds round
  • Public Forum (PF) · 2-on-2 cases, rebuttals, and crossfire
  • Lincoln-Douglas (LD) · 1-on-1 value and criterion debate
  • Policy · tagged evidence, cross-examination, and fast rounds
  • Congressional Debate · floor speeches and questioning
  • Model UN (MUN) · GA, Crisis, Specialized, and JCC committees
  • Karl Popper · prepared 3-on-3 debate with cross-examination
  • Viva / Oral Exam · defend a thesis under live questioning
  • Courtroom · openings, cross-examination, and closings
  • Negotiation · anchors, objections, concessions, and final offers
  • Pitch Defense · pitch, investor questions, and a final close
  • Quick Clash · one constructive and rebuttal per side

What makes this an online debate platform

The motion, speech order, clock, interruptions, and judging criteria stay attached to the round. You do not just post an opinion. You defend it while another side tries to beat it, then get a decision that explains what carried the ballot.

Built by an APDA Pro-Ams champion, 2025. The formats are not one generic prompt with different labels. Policy uses evidence and tagged delivery, LD starts with value and criterion, BP rewards extensions, and APDA stays impromptu without fabricated citations.

Online debate FAQ

Where can I debate online?

Use Spar for a live human opponent, Practice for a typed AI round, Voice Debate for a spoken AI round, or Live Rooms to organize a scheduled match.

Can I debate real people online?

Yes. Spar matches people who are in the queue, while Live Rooms handles scheduled rounds for clubs, classes, and friends. If no human opponent is available, the AI practice modes are ready on demand.

How do I practice debate online without a partner?

Choose typed practice or a live voice round. The AI takes the other side, follows the selected format, handles POIs where the format allows them, and argues through the full round.

Which debate formats are supported?

There are 15 modes: Asian Parliamentary, BP, APDA, Worlds, Public Forum, Lincoln-Douglas, Policy, Congress, Model UN, Karl Popper, Viva, Courtroom, Negotiation, Pitch Defense, and Quick Clash.

Are the rounds voice, video, or text?

All three. Human matches use live video. AI practice can be typed or spoken. Scheduled rooms use live video, with the motion, format, and judge attached to the room.

How are online debates judged?

An AI judge writes a ballot after the round. It names the winner, explains the reason for decision, assigns speaker points where the format uses them, and gives one drill for the next round.
