Online debate FAQ

What is an online debate?

An online debate is a structured argument held by video, voice, or text. On Debatable, each round has a motion, opposing sides, timed speeches, direct rebuttal, and a written judge ballot at the end.

What is the best online debate platform?

The best choice depends on how you want to debate. Debatable supports live video against people, voice or typed rounds against AI, scheduled rooms, 15 formats, and written judge ballots. If you prefer text-only discussion or argument mapping, use the online debate platform comparison to choose a better fit.

Where can I debate online?

Use Spar for a live human opponent, Practice for a typed AI round, Voice Debate for a spoken AI round, or Live Rooms to organize a scheduled match.

Can I debate real people online?

Yes. Spar matches people who are in the queue, while Live Rooms handles scheduled rounds for clubs, classes, and friends. If no human opponent is available, the AI practice modes are ready on demand.

How do I practice debate online without a partner?

Choose typed practice or a live voice round. The AI takes the other side, follows the selected format, handles POIs where the format allows them, and argues through the full round.

Can I interrupt the AI?

Yes. In a live voice round, start speaking to cut the AI off mid-sentence. Debatable marks the interruption in the transcript, adds it to the round count, and passes that context to the judge.

Which debate formats are supported?

Start with informal General Clash in Quick Clash mode, or choose Asian Parliamentary, BP, APDA, Worlds, Public Forum, Lincoln-Douglas, Policy, Congress, Model UN, Karl Popper, Viva, Courtroom, Negotiation, or Pitch Defense.

Are the rounds voice, video, or text?

All three. Human matches use live video. AI practice can be typed or spoken. Scheduled rooms use live video, with the motion, format, and judge attached to the room.

How are online debates judged?

An AI judge writes a ballot after the round. It names the winner, explains the reason for decision, assigns speaker points where the format uses them, and gives one drill for the next round.

Do I need an account to debate online?

An account is optional at the start. Sign in when you want to save your ballots, build a persistent record, and put your speaker points on the public leaderboard. Nothing goes public unless you choose it.
