Why argue out loud when a machine can write it for you?

Anyone can produce a fluent paragraph now, so a fluent paragraph proves nothing. What still shows what you actually think is saying it out loud, to someone who disagrees, on a clock, and taking the answer. Nobody can do that part for you.

That is why this is an online debate site and not another writing tool. The person across from you is a person. The words are yours. The clock is real. And when it ends, somebody tells you plainly whether it worked.

Not more AI slop

Nothing on this site is generated content. Every round is two people, live, arguing a question one of them picked.

The AI is the referee, not the performer

It never argues for you. It reads the round, calls it, and writes out every step of its reasoning, so you can argue with the decision too.

The board only moves when a round happens

Win and you rise on a public leaderboard anyone can read. No padded numbers, no fake activity, no bots in the queue.

It is a game, and the score is real

A real opponent, a real clock, a decision you can read. Win rounds and you climb. Lose one and the decision tells you exactly which argument cost you, so the next round is different.

Online debate FAQ

What is an online debate?

An online debate is a structured argument held over video or voice instead of in a room. On Debatable each round has one question, two people on opposite sides, timed turns, and a written decision from the judge at the end.

Where can I debate online?

Here. Press Debate someone now to join the live queue and get matched with a real person on video. If nobody is waiting, you can take a voice round against the AI while you wait.

Is this like Omegle, but for debate?

Close. You are matched with a stranger on video, but instead of aimless chat you get one shared question, opposite sides, a clock, and a written decision at the end.

Can I debate random people online?

Yes, that is the whole loop. The queue pairs you with whoever is waiting, you argue opposite sides, and the judge decides.

Do I have to show my face?

No. Every live round has an Avatar mode, so your opponent, the audience, and any replay see an animated mask instead of you.

How are online debates judged?

An AI judge writes its decision after the round: who won, why, and a score for each side out of 100. The reasoning is written out in full so you can check it.

Do I need an account to debate online?

Live video requires sign-in with Google, Apple, or email before you enter the queue. You answer three quick matching questions first. Your answers carry through sign-in. Both accept, then meet. Watching is open to anyone.

Can I practice debate online without a partner?

Yes. Take a voice round against the AI. It argues the other side and the same judge writes the decision.
