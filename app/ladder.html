How a share settles. A long share pays the debater's Debate Rating at season close, mapped onto a fixed band (1200 pays nothing, 1900 pays the full 100). A short share pays the remainder, so a matched pair always pays exactly 100 between them and the book cannot owe more than it holds. If there is no rating to settle against, the market stays open and nobody is paid. There is no simulated close and no fallback outcome.

Pricing. An automated market maker quotes both sides at all times, so you can always enter or exit without waiting for someone to take the other side. Bigger orders move the price against you, which is why the confirm screen shows the average price you will actually pay rather than the headline one.

No rake. The market maker subsidizes each book by a fixed, known amount and takes nothing from any trade or settlement. We earn the same whoever turns out to be good. The same fee-neutrality applies to the judge that produces the results underneath: see the judge charter.

Who cannot trade. Debaters are excluded from their own market: they decide that outcome by competing, which is control, not insight. Staking is 18+ and is unavailable where it is not permitted. Points are free, non-purchasable, non-transferable, and non-redeemable. No purchase necessary. Void where prohibited.
