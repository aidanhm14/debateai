    Quick answers

    Can spectators bet on who is winning?

    Inside a live room, signed-in spectators age 18 or older in eligible regions can bet free play tokens on Pro or Con before the middle speech. The AI judge's ballot settles the market. Play tokens cannot be bought, transferred, redeemed, or cashed out.

    Do I need an account to watch?

    For a full round, yes. Playing a published replay and taking a viewer seat both require a free account. A tournament stream playing on this page does not.

    Are these real people or AI?

    The published rounds are real people arguing against each other. The judge that writes the result is AI.

    Can I watch debates that already happened?

    Yes. Ordinary live-room recording is opt in. Tournament rounds are recorded, with preliminary footage private by default. Only recordings cleared for publication appear here.

    How long is a round?

    Replay cards show the total running time. In a live room, the clock for each speech stays visible.

    Can I take part instead of just watching?

    Yes. Start a round and you get the same clock and written ballot.
