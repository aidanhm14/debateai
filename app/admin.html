What the metrics mean
DAU / WAU / MAU
Daily / weekly / monthly active users.
Stickiness
DAU divided by MAU. The share of monthly users who show up on a given day. Roughly 20% or higher is strong.
Cohort / retention
Users grouped by the week they signed up, and the share who came back in later weeks. W1 is the first week after signup.
KPI
Key performance indicator. The headline number cards at the top.
RFD
Reason for decision. The judge's written ballot at the end of a round.
Corpus sample / Preference pack
Data exports. An anonymized set of opted-in rounds, and chosen-versus-rejected pairs used to train AI.

How to read Mission Control

Everything you need to drive the dashboard in one screen.

0 Six workspaces

Pulse is what is happening now. Growth is acquisition, retention and every running A/B. People is individual humans rather than aggregates. Product is what the rounds themselves look like. Broadcast is going live and publishing replays. Ops is the write actions.

Press 1 through 6 to jump between them, or link straight to one with /admin#growth. Your last workspace is remembered per browser.

A workspace only pulls its own endpoints, and only the first time you open it. That is deliberate: opening this page used to fetch all seventeen endpoints at once, which is most of why it kept hitting the Firestore daily read ceiling. ↻ Refresh re-pulls the workspace you are looking at.

The strip under the KPIs is Needs attention: failing endpoints, stale nightly jobs, quarantined corpus rows, contested clash mappings. A red dot on a tab means something in that workspace wants looking at.

1 The time window

The bar at the top has four spans: 24h, 3d, 7d, 30d. Pick one and every time-windowed panel re-pulls for exactly that span. Your choice is remembered on this browser, so a reload keeps it.

2 What moves with the window

These panels all read the window you pick:

  • Hour · day heatmap
  • Format · brain mix
  • Devices
  • Browsers
  • Top pages
  • Power users
  • Round-complete funnel
  • Founder video

The heatmap, mix, devices, browsers and top pages all come from one query, so they always agree on the span.

3 What stays put

A few panels sit on a different axis and ignore the window on purpose:

  • KPI strip (top row) is lifetime totals. "+N last 7d" on Total users is a fixed 7-day delta.
  • Activity chart switches grain with its own daily / weekly / monthly tabs, not the window.
  • Live activity always shows the latest 60 events, streaming.
  • Weekly cohorts are measured in weeks. use its 8w / 12w tabs.

4 Reading the key panels

  • Round-complete funnel: starts vs completes, plus the same rate weighted by unique subjects so a few power users can't inflate it. A "subject" is one signed-in account OR one anonymous DEVICE. Anonymous rows were keyed per browser tab until 2026-08-10, so anything spanning that date counts tabs on the earlier side and devices on the later one. Unique anon counts step down at that date on purpose.
  • Founder video: play → 25 → 50 → 75 → complete drop-off. retention% is each bucket over plays.
  • Heatmap: darker red = a busier UTC hour. good for spotting when your users actually show up.
  • Power users: ranked by engagement score in the window. click a row for that user's full session inspector.

5 Freshness

The dashboard is a snapshot, not a live stream. Auto-refresh is off by default to save Firestore reads. hit ↻ Refresh for a fresh pull, or flip Auto-refresh on to poll every 5 minutes. The live activity tail updates on its own.
