Where do the winnings come from?

From the tokens in that round's pool. The winning side divides the whole pool in proportion to each person's stake. There is no house fee. Returns are rounded down to whole tokens.

Can I buy or withdraw play tokens?

No. Play tokens are free, cannot be transferred, and cannot be redeemed for money or prizes. Paid voice tokens cover AI usage only and never enter a bet.

What does the judge decide?

Who made the winning case in the round. Audience bets never decide the ballot. The recorded judgment settles the bet. Read the judge's published rules.
