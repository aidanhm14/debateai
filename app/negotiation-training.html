What to work on

  • Know what matters to each side

    Ask about priorities and constraints before proposing a compromise.

  • Make a trade, not just a concession

    Connect what you give to something you need in return.

  • Make the agreement specific

    State the scope, timing, and next step both sides would accept.

After the conversation
