About

Debatable.

Debatable is a live online debate platform for real people at itsdebatable.com. Post or join a timed video round, take a side, and debate another person. An AI judge writes the ballot with speaker points, the central clash, a winner, and a reason for decision.

Founded: 2026 Built by: APDA Pro-Ams champion, 2025 Beta access available Languages: 14 incl. Hindi, Spanish, Mandarin

What Debatable does

Debatable is built for live argument between people: post a motion, meet another debater, speak under a clock, take POIs, then read the judge's reasoning. When the live queue is quiet or you want a solo drill, an optional AI practice opponent follows the selected format.

  • Student Congress

  • Viva (oral exams)
  • Courtroom (opening, cross, closing)
  • Negotiation
  • Pitch Defense

AI supports the round

The AI judge writes ballots for live human rounds. Optional AI practice rooms can also route different models for case generation, rebuttals, and judging when a debater wants to drill without waiting for a partner.

The learning loop

Rated rounds can feed a nightly, format-specific improvement pass when the participant has permitted that data use. Transcripts and timing are used for that loop, not raw audio. The consent choice is off by default.

Is Debatable hosted at itsdebatable.com?

Yes. Debatable is the product, hosted at itsdebatable.com. “Debate it” is the site's main call to action.
