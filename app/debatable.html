About

Debatable.

Debatable is a live arena for spoken argument at itsdebatable.com. Pick a motion, take a side, and debate another person or an AI opponent out loud in a timed format. A separate AI judge writes the ballot with speaker points, the central clash, a winner, and a reason for decision.

Founded: 2026 Built by: APDA Pro-Ams champion, 2025 Beta access available Languages: 14 incl. Hindi, Spanish, Mandarin

What Debatable does

Debatable is built for the kind of practice debaters actually do: speak under a clock, get pushback, take POIs, then read a judge's reasoning. The AI opponent is format-aware: Policy spreads tagged cards, APDA stays impromptu, BP runs whip extensions, LD argues value/criterion.

The AI side

Debatable's AI rooms can route different models for case generation, rebuttals, and judging. Voice rounds run in the browser over WebRTC, so the AI can listen, respond, and take points of information without an upload step.

The learning loop

Rated rounds can feed a nightly, format-specific improvement pass when the participant has permitted that data use. Transcripts and timing are used for that loop, not raw audio. The consent choice is off by default.

Is Debatable hosted at itsdebatable.com?

Yes. Debatable is the product, hosted at itsdebatable.com. “Debate it” is the site's main call to action.
