Using Apple Calendar, Outlook, or another calendar?

Apple Calendar opens a calendar subscription. Choose iCloud to show it across your Apple devices. If the button does not open Calendar, use Download .ics to import the session, or copy the download link into Calendar → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar.

Outlook and other calendar apps can import the .ics file. Review your calendar's alert settings after adding it; downloaded events include a reminder 10 minutes before the start.
