Questions

Ask us a question.

How does matching work?

We look among people available now. You can set preferences or meet the first available person. Both people accept before joining. If it is quiet, keep searching or invite a friend.

Can I keep the notes?

Yes. Open a saved round from your profile to read its transcript, notes, and decision. Use Export to Google Docs to keep a copy.

What if I disagree with the decision?

Either side can appeal within 72 hours on the published grounds. A person reviews the appeal. Read the scoring rules and appeal policy.

What does it cost?

There is a Free plan. Private judging includes two free uses per account, then requires a paid plan. See prices and allowances.

What does “This House” mean?

It is an older way of phrasing a debate topic. “This House would ban X” asks whether X should be banned. For supports the ban; Against opposes it.
