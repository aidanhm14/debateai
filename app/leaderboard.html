What do the numbers mean?

Read the short version

On the head-to-head ladder the number is a rating. It moves when you win or lose against real people, and beating stronger opponents moves it more. A rating stays provisional until enough rated rounds narrow it down, which takes a run of them rather than a fixed number. On the AI board the number is the judge's score for how well you argued.

Debated before? Import your Tabroom record and start with a seeded rating instead of a blank 1500. Curious how the number works? The Debate Rating, explained.
