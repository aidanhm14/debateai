How this works. Preview any live round in the monitor. Go on air captures the monitor (this tab, with its audio) and publishes it into the broadcast as the program feed: the site player shows it full-bleed and any host camera rides picture-in-picture. Clicking another round cuts instantly; the capture never restarts. If a round is silent, click the sound button inside the monitor once. Wear headphones, or mute your studio microphone while a round is on air, so the round's audio does not echo through your mic. Rooms marked Do not stream are preliminary tournament rooms whose recordings are private; this desk refuses to put them on air. The broadcast recording lands hidden on /admin until you publish it.
