How a 2v2 runs here

Four people, two benches. Speeches alternate between the benches, and within a bench they alternate between partners, so each of you speaks and only one of you holds the clock at a time. The AI judge writes the ballot at the end. APDA and Quick Clash run as true two on two. Asian Parliamentary is three a side in the wild and Worlds and BP are four teams of two, so those run here as a compressed version rather than the full format.
