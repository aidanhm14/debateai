How it works
  1. Stripe collects the details, not us. You set up a Stripe account from the button above. Your bank details and ID go to Stripe directly. Debatable never sees them and never stores them.
  2. Win a round. The verdict marks the pot as owed to you.
  3. The appeal window runs. Either debater can appeal a verdict. Nothing is sent while that window is open, or while an appeal is being decided.
  4. The pot is sent. Once the window closes with no appeal open, it transfers to your account.

If you win before setting this up, nothing is lost. The pot stays owed to you and sends as soon as your account is ready.

Payouts are 18 or over. A cash round is a contest of skill between the two people paying to compete, and the platform fee is taken when you enter, never out of the pot. The full rules.
