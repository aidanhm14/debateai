01Who is running this

The season is run by the operator of Debatable at itsdebatable.com (the "operator"). Questions, disputes, and anything not covered below go to aidandavidhollinger@gmail.com.

The prize pool for Season 1 is funded by the operator. It is not funded by entry fees, because there are none, and it is not funded out of any prediction pool. Settlement on this site carries no rake, fee, or house account, and the build fails if one is added, so the operator earns the same amount whoever wins. That is verifiable at judge integrity.

02Who can enter

  • Anyone with an account can compete and appear in the standings, at any age, anywhere.
  • Cash prizes go to entrants aged 18 or over at the start of the season. Entrants under 18 compete, rank, and appear on the public standings exactly like everyone else, and are not eligible to receive money.
  • Open worldwide except where prohibited by law. TBD: counsel names the excluded jurisdictions. The operator may exclude a jurisdiction at any time if the law there requires it.
  • The operator, and anyone who worked on the judging system, cannot win a prize. They may compete for the standings.
  • One account per person. Competing on more than one account forfeits every entry.

Why under 18 can win the board but not the money

Paying cash to a minor pulls in a different set of rules than this season is built for, and the honest fix is to not do it rather than to quietly exclude younger debaters from the competition. They rank, they appear, they can win the season outright on the public standings. The money moves down to the next eligible entrant.

03How to enter, and the free path

Complete five judged rounds inside the season window. That is the whole entry requirement. Rounds count whether they are argued against another person or against the AI opponent, in any supported format.

There is no entry fee and no purchase of any kind. A paid subscription does not add standing, does not add rounds toward the five, and does not change how a round is judged. Prediction points cannot be converted into an entry, a round, or a prize.

A round counts only if it completed and received a ballot. Abandoned rounds, rounds where the transcript is empty, and rounds voided under section 05 do not count toward the five.

04How the winner is decided

This is a contest of skill. Standings come from how well entrants argued, judged against criteria published before the season starts.

Each judged round produces a ballot with speaker points. An entrant's season score is the average speaker points across their best five rounds in the window. Rounds beyond five neither help nor hurt, so nobody can win by volume alone and nobody is punished for a bad day after they qualify.

Ties

Broken in this order, stated in advance so nothing is decided after the fact: (1) more judged rounds completed in the window, (2) higher average across all rounds rather than the best five, (3) whoever reached their qualifying fifth round first. If entrants are still level, the prize for those places is split evenly between them.

The judging

Ballots are written by the site's judging panel: three models from three different families, voting against a rubric that is published and fingerprinted before the round, so nobody can retune the standard afterward. Majority carries. An even split is never broken by the operator; that round is void and does not count. The full charter, the rubric hash, and the panel's published agreement figures are at judge integrity.

05Voided rounds, appeals, and disqualification

A round is void and excluded from standings if the panel splits evenly, if the transcript is missing or truncated, or if the operator finds the round was manipulated.

Either debater can appeal a ballot within 72 hours on the published grounds. Appeals are decided by a person, never by a model, and anyone who debated in the round cannot review it. The original ballot is kept beside any correction. An overturned ballot updates the standings.

Grounds for disqualification: more than one account, agreeing with another entrant to throw rounds, feeding the judge instructions that name a winner, or using another person to argue rounds under your account. The operator decides these, states the reason, and will publish it.

06How prizes are paid

  • Winners are contacted at the email on their account within 7 days of the season closing, and have 30 days to respond and confirm eligibility.
  • Payment method: TBD, likely PayPal or bank transfer. The operator confirms identity and age before paying, and does not need it before then.
  • An unclaimed prize passes to the next eligible entrant in the standings.
  • Prizes are paid in US dollars. Anyone outside the US bears any conversion or transfer fee their own bank charges.
  • Winners are responsible for any tax they owe. At $250 the top prize sits under the US reporting threshold, so no tax form is expected for a single win, and this is not tax advice.
  • No prize can be transferred to someone else. The operator may substitute a prize of equal value if paying cash is not possible in a winner's country.

07Standings, privacy, and publication

Season standings are public while the season runs. They show the display name on the account, the season score, and rounds completed. They do not show an email, a location, or an age.

Entering means agreeing that a display name and season result can be published. Anyone who would rather not appear can say so and be removed from the public standings while staying eligible for a prize.

Round transcripts stay governed by the existing privacy policy. Entering a season does not make a private round public.

08The rest

The operator may cancel or shorten the season if it cannot be run fairly, and will say why. If the season is cancelled after entries open, the full prize pool is still awarded to the standings as they stand at cancellation. Void where prohibited.

These rules can be corrected before the season opens. Once it opens, the version published at the start governs the whole season, and any later change appears beside the original rather than replacing it, the same way a corrected ballot does.

This is a contest of skill on judged debate rounds. It is not a lottery, not a sweepstakes drawn by chance, and not a wagering service. No purchase or payment of any kind can enter, improve a standing, or increase a prize.
