How the match is made

01

One take you would defend, the topics you want, and a matching rule. Or press Skip and meet someone now.

03

04

Argue

A simple clock runs the round. Camera, avatar, or no camera.

05

Read the decision

Who won, why, both sides scored out of 100, and a rated record that moves.

You already know the worst version of the other side. This is where you meet the best version, said by someone who believes it, and find out whether your own argument holds up.

Why a judge who ignores the side changes the argument

You cannot win by being right.

The AI judge follows a published rubric: claims, warrants, responses, weighing. It scores how well each side argued, not which position it prefers. So the person who dunks loses to the person who answered.

You cannot dodge the question.

The motion draft means you argue something both of you accepted. No moving the goalposts, no picking a fight the other side never agreed to have.

The clock ends the shouting.

Timed speeches mean both people get the floor and nobody can filibuster. When the clock stops, the round stops, and the decision comes back in writing.

It goes on your record.

A rating in the thousands, like chess. Beat someone stronger and it moves more. The people who keep debating people who disagree with them are the ones who climb.

What we will not run

Disagreement is the point. Being blindsided is not. Abortion and reproductive policy, sexual or domestic violence, suicide and self-harm, child abuse, graphic violence, capital punishment, and genocide are off the motion bank everywhere on the site, including custom motions. There is plenty left to fight about: immigration, billionaires, moderation, meritocracy, climate, policing, school, work, money, culture.

Safety in a round with a stranger

Your face is your call.

Avatar mode sends an animated mask that moves with you instead of your camera. Or turn the camera off. The round runs either way.

Report and block are one tap.

A block is an account relationship, not a device setting. The matchmaker checks it in both directions before it seats anyone. How we keep rounds safe.

Questions people ask

How do I find someone who disagrees with me to debate?

Open the live queue, pick Viewpoint clash when it asks how you want to be matched, and you are paired with another person who also asked to be challenged. The motion draft then puts you on opposite sides of a question you both accepted.

What if I do not want to answer questions first?

Press Skip. You get the first available person and a motion from the draft. The questions make the match sharper; they never gate it.

Does the judge take a side?

No. It scores how well each side argued against a published rubric. The rubric, the model panel, and the human appeal path are at /judge-integrity.

Do I have to show my face?

No. Avatar mode sends an animated mask instead of your camera, and you can turn the camera off entirely.

Are there topics you will not run?

Yes. Abortion, sexual or domestic violence, suicide and self-harm, child abuse, graphic violence, capital punishment, and genocide are off the bank everywhere.

Is it free?

Live video rounds are free after a Google or phone sign-in. The AI opponent gives you a free round without an account. Paid plans start at $10 a year. Pricing.

How is this different from Policon?

Policon matches on a 24-statement political survey and runs voice only. Debatable matches on a shorter profile, runs on video with an optional avatar, drafts the motion before the round, and adds a rated record, replays, and cash tournaments. Full comparison at /vs/policon.
