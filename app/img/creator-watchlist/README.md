## Rules for this directory

Every file here is Creative Commons or public domain, credited above with a
link to its licence and its source. That is the bar and there is no exception
to it: an unlicensed photo of a real person does not go on any surface of this
site. Do not add a file scraped from a profile, a stream, a thumbnail or a
screenshot, however small or however briefly it is shown.

Several of these are share-alike (BY-SA). The crops in this directory are
adaptations, so if a crop is redistributed on its own it carries the same
licence as its source. Crediting in this file, as above, is what keeps the
on-page use compliant.

Nobody pictured here has agreed to anything. These portraits illustrate who a
bounty COULD be aimed at, never who has accepted one. A person shown here must
never be captioned with a record, a score, a ballot, a quote or a scheduled
round, and must never appear on a bounty that names them as a target until
they have accepted it in their own account. When someone genuinely accepts,
their name comes off this speculative list and onto the real one.

Living people only. Check before adding: a "who should we get" list is a
grotesque place to find someone who has died.
